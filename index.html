How it works

  1. Creator sets the plan

    A funding goal, a deadline and milestones, each with a share of the money.

  2. Backers fund in BOT

    Funds sit in the contract. Nobody, including the creator, can touch them yet.

  3. Backers approve work

    Votes are weighted by the amount given. Over half must approve each milestone, in order.

  4. Paid or refunded

    Approved milestones pay out. If the goal is missed, every backer can claim a refund.
