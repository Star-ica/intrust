How it works

📋

Creator sets the plan

A goal, a deadline, and milestones — each with a share of the funds.

🔒

Backers fund in BOT

All funds lock inside the contract. Nobody can touch them yet.

🗳

Backers vote on work

Votes are weighted by contribution. Over 50% approval unlocks each milestone.

✅

Paid or refunded

Approved milestones pay the creator. If the goal is missed, every backer gets a full refund.
